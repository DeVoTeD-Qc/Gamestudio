## The 6 checks (any "yes" = regenerate; two tries, then skip and say so)

1. Any letters, numbers, signs, logos, flags, insignia, patches or a red cross?
2. A visible face, a real person, or a real weapon / vehicle model?
3. Blood, wounds, gore, a skull?
4. A **Tactic or Field** with a person as the subject (or any person where the prompt says none)?
5. Shrunk to a thumbnail strip (160x40), is the subject unclear? Is it outside the middle band?
6. Are the left and right edges bright or busy (the game's icons sit there)?
