Tap a hand card to select it (it animates; landing slots show ghosts), then tap a highlighted column, or drag it onto a column. Long-press (or right-click) any card for the focus view. On the desktop size, hover a banner to zoom it in place. Placeholder art is CSS/SVG made here; the red dot marks a banner that is animating. The stage is drawn on Godot's logical canvas (1168x540 phone, 1280x800 desktop) and scaled to the device size, exactly as screen_profile.gd does. Layout v2 (board-layout-v2.md): the board takes the full height (phone banners 228x57 instead of 176x44), the hand is a side tray that is open in Deploy and closes to a tab in the Firefight (Phase toggle), and the rails become an icon strip on the left: tap a badge for its flyout, long-press for its rules.
