Card Designs SI-04

The head: The cards on board should look exactly like calling cards. Then I'm wondering when they are in hand or mulligan or pre game view, how they should look. Maybe we should simply not put the art filling the entire card to leave space for name and more visibility for icons and stuff. Below: four board looks and four everywhere-else looks, each drawn at the real phone size (844x390). Long press or right click any card to inspect it.

On the board

Jump to the C family (pure calling card, C1-C5) · the B family (side rails, B1-B4). Each banner slot is 228x57 px on the phone canvas (165x41 pt). The art is the calling card itself: no bands and no dimming on it. Each option moves the gameplay info to a different place. Under each board: the states (damaged, Recruit, selected, can't act, enemy Standard Issue) at 2x.

Everywhere else

Hand tray, mulligan, pre-game deck view, Codex and reward picks. Here the art is a window, so the name and icons get clean space. Each option is shown in the phone hand tray (362 px wide) and at mulligan size on the full phone screen. Every name fits whole, ARMORED TROOPER and RECON SPECIALIST included (smallest name 11 pt).

Recommendation

The head's direction: C1+, progressive disclosure. Build C1+ on the board and keep 5+ (the C1+ banner with everything shown, plus a name plate) in the hand, because the hand is where you decide and the cost and traits matter there. At rest a board card shows 3-5 marks instead of 6-9. Ship "Show everything" as a setting (Settings > Cards), on for players who want it.

Pure calling card (C family): C1, corner badges. The full art, uncropped, with exactly the head's anatomy in the four corners: trait top-left, keywords top-right, FP / Armor bottom-right on 20 px plates, and the cost bottom-left (drop it and nothing else moves). It shows every required piece at a readable size and keeps the middle 60% of the art clear. Runner-up: C2, border frame, if the board should look most like a framed calling card and can live without the cost. Pairs with 5, the C1 twin, in the hand tray (the card you hold is the card that lands) and with 2, banner over panel, at mulligan / reward / Codex size.

Side rails (B family): B4, one right rail, is the best B: the art runs full height and nothing ever covers it, and all gameplay sits in one right-hand grid that cannot collide (2 traits, weapon, Recruit level, keywords, '+n' when full). It does not beat C1: it crops 30% of the calling card's width, while C1 keeps the whole calling card and still shows every required icon. Pick B4 over C1 only if a clean, uncovered art matters more than seeing the whole calling card. B4 pairs with 2 (banner over panel) everywhere else; a "B4 twin" for the tray would be the B4 banner plus a name plate, as option 4 is for B.

If the head keeps the A-D set: B, side rails. The calling card stays a true, untouched 4:1 banner; cost, trait and weapon ride a slim left rail, FP and Armor a right rail, both on solid ground, so the numbers read at a glance on any art (16-20 px plates, 11.6-14.5 pt). Abilities sit in one small tab on the art's top-right edge.

Everywhere else: 4, the board twin, in the hand tray; 2, banner over panel, at mulligan / reward / Codex size. In the tray the card you hold is the exact banner that lands, plus a name plate, so there is nothing new to learn between hand and board, and 8 cards still fit. At the bigger sizes the full calling card sits on top and a solid panel carries the full name and the icons.

Fonts: Barlow Condensed (names), Stardos Stencil (Standard Issue names and serial plates only), Share Tech Mono (numbers), as in the game. Art: our own public placeholder art (Killstreak card art), never the licensed MW3 set.
