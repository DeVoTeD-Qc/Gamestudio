# Visual polish pass v01: art audit and plan

*Author: Sr Game Artist Agent · 2026-11-03 · Board task v01 · Target: Godot 4.4.1 web, Compatibility renderer*
*Primary device: iPhone landscape 844×390 pt @3x (1168×540 canvas, 0.722 pt per px).*

## 1. Audit (before screenshots: `qa/screenshots/v01-before-*.png`)

Ranked by how much each one hurts the look at phone size:

1. **Card art windows are empty.** Every CardView (inspector, Supply Drop, Reward) shows a grey
   icon floating in a black box (a single weapon shape or a heart-rate line). That box is the largest
   area of the card, so the cards look unfinished.
2. **The board is a flat, empty grid.** The three Port Vasel zones are identical dark rectangles. The
   eight opaque, filled empty slots per lane read as spreadsheet cells. Nothing says "container port",
   and units don't stand out from empty space.
3. **Unit tokens are text-only.** Tokens have a blank middle third between the name and FP/AR. You
   can't tell a sniper from a shield at a glance without reading the 20 px class icon.
4. **The Title is plain text on black.** It has no key art, no logo treatment, and nothing that sets
   the mood before the first tap.
5. **Run screens are flat.** The run map, Loadout and Supply Drop are boxes on solid slate. The five
   match cards on the run map are mostly empty space.
6. **Buttons and panels have no depth.** They are flat outlines with the same weight everywhere. READY
   (the main call to action) does not glow or pop, and pressed states have no physical feel.
7. **Killfeed clipping (bug p18-005).** On the phone the killfeed lives in a narrow top-bar slot of
   about 180 pt, so lines are cut to "BREACH ▸ KES" and the victim can't be read.

What already works and stays as it is: the palette (slate, gunmetal, amber, teal, red, cyan), the
fonts, friend and enemy shape cues (chevron notch versus hazard stripes), the card frame layout and
the 11 pt / 44 pt phone floors.

## 2. Plan and how it is built (all original, no downloads, no image generation)

| # | Upgrade | Technique | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | **Card art.** One original duotone vector illustration per card, covering 21 deck cards, 5 killstreaks, 4 gear items and 3 operator weapons: helmeted contractor silhouettes (faceless, generic gear), weapons, tactics and gadgets on a dusk port backdrop. Unit tokens on the board also get a faint class silhouette. | SVG strings rasterised at runtime with `Image.load_svg_from_string()` at the scale on screen (crisp at 3x, about 0 bytes of texture data in the pck) | `scripts/ui/art/card_art.gd` (generated by `tests/tools/gen_card_art.py`), `card_view.gd`, `unit_slot.gd` |
| b | **Port Vasel board.** Each lane gets a low-contrast location backdrop: CONTAINER STACKS (stacked boxes, twist-lock lines), CRANE YARD (gantry cranes, rails), LOADING OFFICES (low office block, roller doors, windows). Empty slots become see-through HUD bracket frames, so the place shows through and units stand out. | SVG backdrop per lane (runtime raster) plus a nine-patch bracket StyleBoxTexture | `lane.gd`, `build_themes.gd` |
| c | **Title and run key art.** A dusk Port Vasel skyline (cranes, stacks, floodlights, water) behind the Title, with an original wordmark treatment (amber slash bars and chevrons). A shared tactical backdrop shader (grid, vignette, grain) goes behind every run screen and the match. | SVG key art plus `assets/shaders/tactical_backdrop.gdshader` | `Title.tscn` / `title.gd`, `run_screen.gd`, `Match.tscn` |
| d | **Buttons and panels.** A drop shadow "lip" for depth, a pressed state that sinks 2 px, a brighter hover state, a soft amber glow on READY and on the primary buttons, and slightly see-through panels so the backdrop shows. | StyleBoxFlat shadow, border and margins in the theme pipeline | `tests/tools/build_themes.gd` → `hud_theme.tres` |
| e | **p18-005 killfeed.** On the phone the killfeed moves to a two-line overlay over the hand during the firefight (the hand can't be played then). The overlay is about 520 pt wide, so full names fit. A test checks that lines fit at 844×390. | Code and layout | `match_screen.gd`, `tests/test_board_layout.gd` |

Budget: under 1.5 MB of added assets. The art ships as SVG text inside scripts, which is a few
tens of KB, so the web load stays near its 10 s budget.

**Sizes.** Added source is 385 KB: 329 KB of generated SVG data, a 4.5 KB rasteriser, a 1.8 KB shader and the 50 KB
generator, which is not exported. The web `index.pck` went from 912,256 to 951,600 bytes (+39 KB; GDScript export
compresses the strings) and from 706 KB to 738 KB gzipped. The whole web build went from 44.99 MB to 45.03 MB.
No bitmaps were added.

**Checks.** Tests: 302/302 pass, including the new killfeed test. Import is clean and the export has no errors. Phone floors are
unchanged: 11 pt text and 44 pt taps, with the Title still fitting 540 px including the replace-run confirmation.

## 4. Left for a later pass
- Hand chips still have no art. They are text-dense at phone size, so a thin art strip would need a layout change.
- Loadout, AfterAction and RunEnd only get the backdrop and depth. Pick tiles could show killstreak and perk art.
- The lane art is deliberately faint. Check it on a real iPhone at full brightness and in sunlight, and tune the `lane_*` colours.
- Unit token silhouettes are generic per class. Per-card poses (medic, demolitions, recon pack) exist in the card art and could
  feed the tokens.
- The art is runtime-rasterised SVG. Measure first-open cost on an older iPhone (expected under 5 ms per card, about 40 ms
  for the Title key art at 2x).
- Desktop killfeed names still shorten to 10 characters in the top-bar slot (existing behaviour).
