# DeadDraw: studio head full playthrough, 2026-11-11

*Build: DeadDraw v8 (commit fd12897). Verbatim feedback, then the same points sorted by owner.*

> "DeadDraw does have some serious potential, I just did a full playthrough. I think we need a solid polish pass."

## UX and presentation (UI/UX)
1. The UX could be better. The wave info should be a similar size to the room info.
2. Wave opening and room opening should show **backs of cards flying into the main draw pile** we reveal cards from.
3. Room cost: a nice **locked label on top of the room image/icon** instead of a text box under it.
4. Every weapon needs a nice **icon for the damage it does**.
5. The **zombie kill stack** on a gun should be a bit bigger.
6. Guns should visually show **slots for the zombie stack**, so capacity reads at a glance.
7. **Zombie strength** should render better than plain numbers.
8. **Shorten all tooltip/inspect text**: it's overwhelming.
9. The variety of weapon rules is good; meaningful icons are good. Add a **Help / Glossary button** that shows all of them.
10. More feedback when **a new wave starts** (a banner) and when **a room becomes available** (highlight). Like CoD Zombies, a **clear current-wave indicator**.
11. A **Codex** where we can see all the cards and zombies in the game.
12. Ask the UX designer for **multiple UX mockups**.

## Rules and content (design)
13. Swapping out a gun when stuck (trashing it by equipping another) feels bad: you lose all its points. Maybe keep some points?
14. Perks were liked: we need **more of them**.
15. **Point carry-over into the second mission** felt odd: you could open every room from the start. Maybe don't carry points over? (Not sure.)
16. More variety, e.g. a **big gun / rocket**: super high damage but only one zombie.
17. **Melee** may be too strong once upgraded. Its drawback is that it scores no points, and it was very important when guns can't be completed. Idea: **start with bare hands** and find **melee weapons in the deck** that hit harder but have **durability**, like guns.
18. The **one medkit per turn** rule felt odd.
19. **Running away** should return **all** the cards, not just the zombies.
