KILLINGCARD
//// ART DIRECTION v1 · BOARD TASK KC-DA · PUBLIC ART ONLY

An operator's field case opened at dusk. The case is quiet: matte gunmetal plates, steel rims, khaki orders paper, every label stamped and short. What sits inside is loud: collectible cards painted with drama, the only thing allowed to shout. Teal is us, red is them, amber is "act now". A painted original and a licensed calling card sit in the same case without a seam.

Five pillars

01

Quiet case, loud card

UI is matte, dark, calm. Card art carries all the colour and energy, and is never dimmed or tinted.

02

Briefing room

Orders, stamps, numbered steps, khaki paper for Tactics, mono type for intel. Every screen says what to do now.

03

Calling-card collectible

Each banner is a trophy. Finishes, alt arts and the Elite star are earned and shown off.

04

One colour, one meaning

Teal you. Red rival and danger. Amber act now. Brass Operator. Nothing else carries meaning.

05

Hit, then explain

Strikes land hard in one beat, then the board settles and says plainly what changed.

MaterialRule
Steel plateMatte gunmetal, 1-2 px rim, 45° chamfer on top-left + bottom-right, one soft top-left bevel. No rivets, rust, scratches, grunge.
Orders paperKhaki or light paper, faint fibre, typed mono text, square corners. Where rules and orders live.
Hazard stripesAmber/black = ready to call, drop zone. Red/black = hostile. Edges only, never behind text.
GlowSignals only, signal colour at 30-60%, 6-12 px soft.
Shape = meaningHexagon cost · circle reticle FP · shield plate Armor · chevron level · diamond Field · rounded chip trait.

Icon rules

48 px
32
24 must read
17 survives
tinted you
tinted rival
  1. Bold white pictograms, tinted by code: one icon, every state, both sides.
  2. Read at 24 px, survive at 17 px. 2-3 shapes, filled silhouettes, min 2.5 px stroke at 24 px.
  3. Side or three-quarter top view, compact near-square silhouettes (no thin rotors or long flat wings).
  4. One meaning, one icon: shield = Armor, eye = PREVIEW, reticle = FP. Nothing else borrows them.
  5. No text, letters, numbers, real weapon models, insignia or skulls inside icons.
  6. Icons sit on a dark socket (#12161A, 1 px rim) on art, never straight on a painting.

Prompt template (ChatGPT, landscape 3:2)

Original painted game card illustration, landscape 3:2, 1536x1024.
Subject: [one invented operator / one object / one event], [verb], seen [side / three-quarter], filling the middle.
Style: semi-realistic painting with graphic-novel edges, bold silhouette, visible brushwork, strong warm rim light,
three value groups, slightly saturated, high contrast.
Theatre: [Port dusk: amber sodium lights over a slate-blue sky, cranes and containers | Arctic dawn | Desert haze |
Night city | Jungle rain].
Gear: olive, khaki and charcoal tactical gear, face covered by a mask, goggles or visor, fictional weapon, no markings.
Composition: head and weapon between 30% and 65% of the height, in the middle 70% of the width; left and right 15%
calm, darker, simple; horizontal flow.
No text, letters, numbers, logos, flags, insignia, patches or stamps. No real people, no real weapon or vehicle
models. No blood or gore.

Motion and VFX: call-in, impact, settle

BeatTimeWhat happens
Call-in0.25 s in, 0.6-0.9 s holdSteel banner slides in from the owner's side: teal rim (you) or red hazard rim (rival); icon, name, one line.
Telegraphuntil resolveOne persistent marker in the owner's colour on the target (a hatch or pin, not a full-column fill).
Impact0.1-0.3 sHit-stop 50-80 ms, 4-8 px shake on the struck column only, white flash max 2 frames, warm sparks and smoke.
Numbers0.4 sDamage pops 1.3 → 1.0, red / green, Barlow Condensed.
Settle0.3 sDowned cards crack and drop; one mono log line; the board is still again.

One big moment at a time, in A → B → C order. Snappy out (ease-out-back 0.25 s), quick in (0.15 s). No full-screen flashes. Reduced motion: fades only, no shake. Particles warm or side-coloured, never rainbow.

Questions for the head

1 · House style
  • Recommended: one painted "Theatre at dusk" house style for base cards; graphic families only for earned alt arts.
  • Fully mixed styles on every card (banner plan v1).
  • One style only, no alt-art families.
2 · Grit
  • Recommended: clean tactical (matte steel; grit only inside the paintings).
  • Grittier worn kit (scratches, dirt, stencil everywhere).
3 · Collectible lean
  • Recommended: medium: finishes, alt arts, binder Codex, equipped banner on title and debrief; no loot-box reveals.
  • Heavy: pack openings, shiny reveals, rarity glows on the board.
  • Light: the collection is a list.
4 · Killstreak colour
  • Recommended: colour by owner (teal / red), kind by icon.
  • Keep a signature hue per killstreak (new non-clashing hues needed).
