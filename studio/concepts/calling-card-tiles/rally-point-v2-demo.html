The four suits: what happens when a hub of that suit rallies

◆ StrikeThe closer gets +2 instead of +1. Tempo: hold the last piece.
● SignalEvery open hub that shares a banner with it gets 1 Charge. Build pots, then cash them.
▲ RidgeNothing extra, but if it never rallies its banners keep the match bonus at the end.
✚ SupplyIts owner draws 1 tile. Flexible: patches, filtering, odd shapes.
Rally scoringEach touching banner cell: 1 point to its owner, 2 if it matches. Closer +1 (Strike +2) plus 1 per Charge. Hub owner gets its Command (+2).
ChargesTokens on a hub. Each one pays +1 to whoever closes it, which may be your opponent.
Your stack12 tiles, 4–5 emblems, at most 2 suits, 2 copies per card (alt arts count as copies). Draw back to 3 each turn.
