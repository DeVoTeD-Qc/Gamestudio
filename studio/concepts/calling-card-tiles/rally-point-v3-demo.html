RallyEach touching banner or insignia cell scores 1 for its owner, 2 if it matches the objective's class. Closer +1 (+1 per Charge). Owner gets its Command. Each touching banner pays its owner 1 materiel of its class, an insignia 2, the objective 1.
CoverA banner or insignia touching another of its owner's Engineer tiles, or its owner's Bunker, is in cover. A Strike can't destroy it unless the attacker spotted first. Objectives and scored points are never destroyed.
Streak+1 per rally you close and per order you fire. An enemy Strike that lands resets it. 3 Spotter Drone (+1 Intel, see their hand) · 5 Supply Drop (draw 1, place it now) · 7 Fire Mission (Front +2 through Armor, destroy an exposed banner).
WinningMost points when the board is full or both players pass. The Front has 13 steps; pushing it to the enemy end is a Breakthrough and wins at once (about 1 game in 12). The second player starts with 1 point.
