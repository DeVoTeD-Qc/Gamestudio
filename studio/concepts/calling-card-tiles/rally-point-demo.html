Before the game

The board and the tiles

Your turnTake 1 of the 3 face-up tiles and place it.
Calling cardA banner covering any 4 empty cells in a straight line.
EmblemA 2×2 hub on any empty 2×2, or a patch over an enclosed gap of 1–4 cells. Patches never score.
RallyWhen all 8 cells around a hub are filled (the board edge counts), every banner touching it scores: 1 per cell, 2 if its suit matches. Whoever closes it gets +1.
Game endThe stack runs out. Hubs that never rallied score what touches them, at 1 per cell. Most points wins.
