Rally Point · the 5 rules

  1. On your turn, place one tile from your hand of 3 on empty squares: a banner (1×4), an objective (2×2) or an insignia (1×1). An objective may also fill a closed-in gap of 1–4 squares, but then it never rallies.
  2. An objective rallies the moment all 8 squares around it are filled. The board edge counts as filled.
  3. When it rallies, every square of a tile touching it scores 1 for that tile's owner, or 2 if the tile's colour matches the objective. Whoever closed it scores 1 more.
  4. Each rally also gives you 1 Ammo for every one of your tiles touching it. At 5 Ammo you Strike (at the end of your turn): remove an enemy tile that touches an open objective. If there is none, score 1.
  5. When the board is full or no one can place, the most points wins. The second player starts with 1 point.

After each turn you draw back up to 3 tiles from your stack. If none of your tiles fit, you swap one with your stack and pass. Right-click or long-press anything on the table to see what it gives you right now.
